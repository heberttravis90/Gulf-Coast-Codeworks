Stack Test Pro: field software built for stack-testing teams.

A specialized field and office workflow designed around the moving pieces of source testing.

Stack testing has a lot of moving pieces: methods, equipment, test locations, calculations, calibration information, run data, crews, client requirements, reports, and last-minute changes.

Stack Test Pro is designed to organize that workflow from mobilization through results instead of scattering it across paperwork, spreadsheets, text messages, and disconnected tools.

Planning and calculation aids support qualified personnel. Final calculations and regulatory decisions still have to be checked against the applicable method, permit, test plan, calibration requirements, and company QA/QC.
Workflow

From mobilization to closeout.

Mobilization & Loadout

Method-aware equipment planning, saved lists, notes, and job preparation.

Setup & Preliminaries

Job setup, measurements, traverse information, test-location details, and method-specific preparation.

Field Testing

Run-level information, calculations, calibration data, CGA, photos, notes, and workflow support.

Calculations

Purpose-built aids for common source-testing calculations and spot checks.

CGA & RATA

Support for calibration information, run comparisons, bias, relative accuracy, and pass/review workflows.

Crew & Job Management

Roles, assignments, saved jobs, shared notes, documents, and operational visibility.
