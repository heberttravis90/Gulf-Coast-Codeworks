Support

Need help with PayForecast? Email gulfcoastcodeworks@gmail.com.

Restore a Pro purchase

Open PayForecast, go to the Pro section, and use Restore purchases. Make sure Google Play is signed in with the same Google account used for the original purchase.

Manage a subscription

Recurring PayForecast Pro subscriptions are billed and managed through Google Play. Use Google Play's subscriptions section to view, change, or cancel a subscription.

Your saved data

Version 1.0 is offline-first. Shift and pay records are stored on the device. Use PayForecast's export or backup feature before replacing or resetting your phone if you want an additional copy.

Pay estimates

PayForecast is an estimator, not payroll, tax, legal, or employment advice. Employer policies, agreements, wage rules, payroll timing, taxes, and reimbursement rules can cause actual pay to differ.

When reporting a problem

Please include your phone model, Android version, PayForecast version, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Only send information needed to troubleshoot the issue.

Privacy
