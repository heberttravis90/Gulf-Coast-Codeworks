Local understanding. Remote capability.

Based in Southeast Louisiana and shaped by real project-management, environmental testing, industrial field operations, QA/QC, and client-facing work, Gulf Coast Code Works approaches software from the operations side first. The question is not “what technology can we sell?” It is “what part of the work is wasting time, creating mistakes, or making the team fight the process?”

Operations & Project Management

Scheduling, job status, crews, documents, notes, approvals, equipment, customer information, and closeout workflows.

Reporting & QA/QC

Excel intake, data validation, dashboards, preliminary reports, exception tracking, trend reporting, and saved history.

Mobile-friendly job tools, field data, photos, checklists, calculations, equipment tracking, and field-to-office handoff.

Small Business Automation

Replace repeat entry, email handoffs, paper forms, and disconnected processes with a structured internal system.

Serving Southeast Louisiana and the Gulf Coast.

Projects can support businesses across the Northshore, Covington, Mandeville, Baton Rouge, New Orleans, the River Parishes, Acadiana, Lafayette, Lake Charles, and elsewhere in Louisiana. Gulf Coast Code Works can work locally when in-person collaboration makes sense and remotely when that is faster for the project.

Built for the business, not the other way around.

Custom software makes the most sense when the operation has a process that matters enough to improve but is too specific for generic software. That might be a contractor tracking jobs and crews, an environmental company handling field data and reporting, a professional office managing documents and approvals, or a growing business that has stretched Excel as far as it can go.

The end product might be an internal web app, a customer or employee portal, a workflow automation system, a dashboard, a reporting tool, a field application, or a specialized industrial platform. The starting point is the same: understand the process, remove the friction, and build only what is useful.
