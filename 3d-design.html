From an idea to a print-ready 3D design.

Custom geometry for printing, prototypes, decor, branding, and useful parts.

Have an idea but no CAD model? Gulf Coast Code Works can turn a description, logo, sketch, reference image, or dimensions into custom 3D geometry developed for additive manufacturing.

What can be designed

Louisiana-inspired decor

Fleur-de-lis, Cajun and Gulf Coast themes, display pieces, and custom home decor.

Business branding

Logos, desk pieces, plaques, displays, promotional concepts, and branded items.

Functional concepts

Holders, organizers, shop accessories, fixtures, simple replacement concepts, and purpose-built designs.

The useful dimensions matter

For functional work, the design starts with the real measurements: what it has to hold, where it has to fit, printer constraints, clearances, wall thickness, and how the finished part will be used. A pretty model that does not fit the real object is not finished.

Typical deliverables

Projects can include STL or other print-ready model files plus a preview render so the geometry can be reviewed before printing. Prototype prints are still valuable because real-world fit and material behavior can require small tolerance adjustments.
