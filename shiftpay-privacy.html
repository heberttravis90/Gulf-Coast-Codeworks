ShiftPay

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

ShiftPay is an offline-first pay and shift estimation app published by Gulf Coast Code Works. This policy explains how information is handled in the app.

Information entered in ShiftPay

ShiftPay can store information you enter about work schedules and estimated pay, including hourly rates, work and travel hours, overtime rules, per diem, mileage, premiums, bonuses, notes, estimated deductions, rotation schedules, and paycheck comparison amounts.

Version 1.0 stores this information locally on your device. Gulf Coast Code Works does not operate a cloud account or server that receives your ShiftPay work records.

Google Play purchases

If you purchase ShiftPay Pro, payment is processed by Google Play. ShiftPay uses Google Play Billing to check whether the Google account on the device has an active subscription or qualifying one-time purchase. Gulf Coast Code Works does not receive or store your full payment card information in the app.

Google's handling of purchase and account information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

ShiftPay version 1.0 does not include third-party advertising, developer analytics, location tracking, contact access, microphone access, camera access, or SMS access.

Exports and backups

ShiftPay lets you export or share data files when you choose to do so. Those files may then be handled by the destination app or service you select. ShiftPay does not automatically transmit those exports to Gulf Coast Code Works.

Your Android device may also include operating-system backup or restore features controlled by your device and Google account settings. Those platform features are not operated by Gulf Coast Code Works.

Support communications

If you contact Gulf Coast Code Works for support, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or exported files. We use support communications to respond to your request and troubleshoot the app.

Children

ShiftPay is intended as a work and pay estimation tool and is not directed to children.

Security and your responsibility

Because ShiftPay stores work and pay information on your device, protect your device with appropriate screen-lock and account security. Avoid sharing exported files with people or services you do not trust.

Changes to this policy

If ShiftPay later adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, pay-stub image processing, or other data-handling features, this policy will be updated before those features are released when required.
