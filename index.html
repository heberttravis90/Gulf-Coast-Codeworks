The Background Behind the Build

Real operations experience, applied to software.

My professional background is rooted in project management, environmental testing and industrial field operations. I’ve spent years coordinating people, equipment, schedules, technical requirements, clients, data quality and deadlines.

That experience is what pushed me toward software development. Too many businesses still rely on disconnected spreadsheets, paper forms, group messages and repetitive manual entry for work that deserves a better system. I build software around the way the operation actually runs instead of forcing the operation to fit somebody else’s software.

I’m continuing to expand my software and web-development work through practical applications, workflow systems, mobile tools, dashboards and business-process automation. My approach is personal and straightforward: understand the problem first, build what’s useful, test it in the real world and keep improving it.
