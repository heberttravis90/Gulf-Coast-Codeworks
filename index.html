Why Gulf Coast Codeworks exists.

Personal experience turned into practical software.

I came into software from the operations side of business. My background includes project management, environmental testing, field operations, technical troubleshooting, scheduling, data quality, client coordination and getting complicated work completed under real deadlines.

That experience made one thing obvious: a lot of businesses are still asking spreadsheets, paper forms and disconnected tools to do jobs they were never meant to do. Information gets entered twice. Teams work from different versions. Managers spend too much time chasing status. And sometimes expensive software makes the process harder instead of easier.

Gulf Coast Codeworks is my way of solving those problems. I build practical web applications, mobile-friendly tools, workflow systems, dashboards and reporting solutions around the way the business actually operates.
