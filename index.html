Why this approach

Software should work the way the business works.

Gulf Coast Codeworks focuses on understanding the real process before deciding what to build. That means looking at the people, the handoffs, the paperwork, the spreadsheets, the calculations, and the parts of the day everybody has simply learned to tolerate.

That approach is especially useful for field operations, technical businesses, service companies, small teams, and growing companies that have outgrown the workarounds that got them this far.
