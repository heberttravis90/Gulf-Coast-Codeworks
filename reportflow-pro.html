The problem

Not every sheet belongs to every job.

Large operational workbooks contain helper sheets, inactive fields, alternate methods, historical tabs, and other material that should not become a false QA finding.

1. Detect the industry

Start with the broad workflow instead of assuming every workbook means the same thing.

2. Identify the job profile

Determine the test or work configuration before deciding what is required.

3. Apply the right rule pack

Mapped requirements can create findings. Uncertain layouts can be marked for review instead of false failure.

Capabilities

One reporting engine, different workflows.

Preliminary client reports

Pull runs, emissions results, standards, pollutant worksheets, comparisons, and status into a clean preliminary package.

Focused QA/QC

Check the information that belongs to the active job and make review items visible before delivery.

RATA support

Support RM-versus-CEMS comparisons, relative accuracy, bias, and related run-level review.

Business reporting

Track projects, status, labor, value, filters, exceptions, trends, and management reporting.

Saved reporting

Keep repeat work, reports, and company data organized instead of rebuilding every package from scratch.

Multi-industry structure

The environmental profile is the first tuned production pack, with a framework that can expand to other operational reporting needs.
