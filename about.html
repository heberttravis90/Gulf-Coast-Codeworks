Hi, I’m Travis

The background behind the build.

My professional background is rooted in project management, environmental testing, and industrial field operations. I have spent years coordinating people, equipment, schedules, technical requirements, clients, data quality, and deadlines.

That experience pushed me toward software development. Too many businesses still rely on disconnected spreadsheets, paper forms, group texts, and repetitive manual entry for work that deserves a better system. I build software around the way the operation actually runs instead of forcing the operation to fit somebody else’s software.

I continue to expand that work through practical applications, workflow systems, mobile tools, dashboards, reporting platforms, and business-process automation. The approach is straightforward: understand the problem first, build what is useful, test it in the real world, and keep improving it.
